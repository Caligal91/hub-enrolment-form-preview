What the research says

Difficulties rarely fix themselves: 88% of children who were poor readers at the end of Grade 1 were still poor readers in Grade 4.

Source: Juel (1988), Journal of Educational Psychology

Children not reading well by the end of Grade 3 were about 4 times more likely to leave school early (US study of almost 4,000 students).

Source: Hernandez (2011), Double Jeopardy, Annie E. Casey Foundation

Teaching sounds and letters directly improved spelling as well as reading.

Source: Ehri et al. (2001), Review of Educational Research

The good news

Clear, step-by-step teaching of sounds and letters works, and works best when it starts early.

Source: Ehri, Nunes, Stahl & Willows (2001), Review of Educational Research

General research, not a diagnosis for Mia.
